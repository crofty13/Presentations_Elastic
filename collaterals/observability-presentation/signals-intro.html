Why one platform

Traces, synthetics and profiles run on the same platform as logs and metrics: same query language, one bill. One platform is not a slogan, it is a TCO argument.

What it means in practice

Every signal is correlated in one place: jump from a trace to its logs, and from a slow service to the profile behind it, without switching tools.
