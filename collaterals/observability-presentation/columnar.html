How 25 bytes became 3.75

Doc-value skippers replace inverted indices and BKD trees. The _id is computed from the series and timestamp instead of stored. Each series stays sequential on disk, so codecs and skippers stay effective.

A general-purpose BI engine pays for its generality on every query. This one does one thing: time-series.
