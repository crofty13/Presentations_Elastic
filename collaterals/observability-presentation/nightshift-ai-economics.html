AI Economics

Token efficiency is a core differentiator.

You can't pass petabytes of raw logs to an LLM. The platform that compresses signal without losing meaning wins: on quality and cost.

What reaches the model

Kilobytes, not gigabytes. Raw telemetry stays in low-cost storage and is queried on demand with ES|QL.

How the layers are built

The KI extractor distills raw telemetry into knowledge indicators, and investigation correlates those into significant events.

The hierarchy IS the token strategy.

Each layer compresses meaning while discarding noise. Agents reason over Significant Events and KIs, not raw logs. This is what makes Elastic's AI economically viable at enterprise scale.
