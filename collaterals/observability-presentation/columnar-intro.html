Why metrics were heavy

A metric is tiny: a timestamp, a value, a few dimensions. A document store wraps it in machinery built for documents: an _id, sequence numbers, inverted index entries and per-field metadata.

And reading it back

Querying meant scanning the full document, effectively a full table scan. The columnar engine reads one column and skips everything else.
