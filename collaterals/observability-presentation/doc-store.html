Match and score

Elastic doesn't just match terms, it scores them. On messy, ever-changing application logs, the most relevant line comes back first.

How it works

Any line comes in: JSON, syslog, raw app output. Fields are extracted automatically and every term lands in an inverted index. New fields are indexed on arrival: no schema migration, no DDL.
