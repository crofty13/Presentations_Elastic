Metrics × AI

AI is causing an explosion in metrics.

Every AI session fans out across LLM calls, GPU cycles, RAG queries, and tool invocations, each generating its own stream of metric series.

Series in
AI metrics volume is non-deterministic by design.
If you store it all

Costs spiral.

Cardinality compounds. Storing it all at standard rates breaks your observability budget.

What's needed

A storage engine purpose-built for high-cardinality metrics, at a cost that scales with the business, not against it.

If you drop data

You go blind.

Drop a series and you break the investigation thread. Missing data makes RCA impossible.

What's needed

Full-fidelity retention across every agent session, so investigation context is always there when an incident hits.

Why the count explodes

Each AI session adds new dimensions: agent ID, model version, tool name, retry count. As adoption scales, the series count explodes, and most metric stores charge per series.

Why dropping data hurts

Investigations follow the full chain of agent calls, tool invocations and model responses. Missing data is not a gap, it is a blind spot.
